AquariumTV Privacy Policy
Last updated: November 3, 2026

AquariumTV is a screensaver for Roku devices, made by Kevin Hoople.

AquariumTV does not collect, store, or share any personal information or usage
data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no
tracking. It does not connect to the internet or any server while it runs. All
of its images are built into the app.

Your Roku device and the Roku platform may collect information on their own,
as described in Roku's privacy policy (https://www.roku.com/legal). That
collection is done by Roku, not by AquariumTV, and is outside its control.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new
"Last updated" date.
